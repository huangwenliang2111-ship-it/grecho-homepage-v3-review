Air-permeable fiberglass coated veil can help sound energy reach an absorptive core when paired with an appropriately engineered acoustic panel.

Sound Reflection & Speech Coverage

THE MATERIAL APPROACH

A low-air-permeability facing can form part of a more reflective ceiling assembly, with final reflection and coverage established through acoustic system design.
