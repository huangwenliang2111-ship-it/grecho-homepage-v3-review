APPLICATION-BASED SOLUTION01 / 04

Moisture & UV Resistance for Exterior Sheathing

Check moisture, interim UV and wall exposure for gypsum sheathing.

THE MATERIAL APPROACH

Specify high-waterproof and UV-resistant fiberglass coated veils developed for gypsum exterior sheathing. Evaluate surface water resistance, UV durability, gypsum-core bonding, and board fabrication performance. Confirm permitted weather exposure periods and compatibility with the complete wall assembly to support reliable installation and long-term performance.
