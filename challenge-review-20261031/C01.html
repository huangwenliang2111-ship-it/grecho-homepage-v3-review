Common problems

Common Surface Appearance Problems

Surface-related issues can arise during coating, drying, lamination, handling, or use. The following are practical signs manufacturers may observe when evaluating fiberglass, fabric, or aluminum foil facers.

01

Uneven Surface Finish

Wrinkles, local waviness, or visible unevenness can interrupt the clean appearance of finished panels and may become more noticeable after lamination or installation.

Explore solution →

02

Inconsistent Coating Distribution

Uneven coating coverage can lead to patchy finishes, variable opacity, and visible differences across the width or length of the facer.

Explore solution →

03

Poor Core Hiding

When the facer does not sufficiently mask the underlying core, fiber patterns or color differences may show through and compromise the finished panel appearance.

Explore solution →

04

Uneven Light Reflection

In directional lighting, localized gloss differences can create bright patches or inconsistent reflections across installed ceilings and wall panels.

Explore solution →

05

Color Inconsistency

Differences in shade between rolls or production batches can become highly visible when panels are installed next to one another.

Explore solution →

06

Surface Defects & Imperfections

Pinholes, microcracks, and other visible imperfections may reduce surface quality and can raise concerns about the integrity of a coated or foil-based facing.

Explore solution →

07

Inconsistent Surface Texture

Variations in woven patterns, coated textures, or surface grain can create an uneven look, particularly in close-view decorative applications.

Explore solution →

08

Surface Discoloration & Aging

Exposure to light and environmental conditions can change the original color or appearance of a facing over time.

Explore solution →

Improving Substrate Uniformity and Web Flatness

Select fiberglass coated veils with consistent thickness and good flatness. Review the base veil construction, coating leveling, drying profile, and lamination tension to reduce visible surface irregularities.

→

GRECHO solution

Establishing Measurable Color Consistency

Stabilize pigment formulation, coat weight, and drying parameters. Use approved color standards with L*a*b* measurements and agreed ΔE tolerances to manage batch-to-batch color differences.

Application scenarios

Application Scenarios

Acoustic Ceiling Panels

Consistent coverage and color help deliver a uniform ceiling appearance across large installed areas. Matte options can also support spaces with demanding lighting conditions.

Acoustic Wall Panels

Decorative wall panels are often viewed up close, making weave consistency, controlled surface texture, and even color especially relevant.

PIR/PUR Thermal Insulation Boards

Foil facers are evaluated for visible surface defects, reinforcement needs, and production-handling robustness as part of the insulation board manufacturing process.

Exterior Gypsum Board Facers

Where outdoor exposure is expected, facer color and appearance should be assessed alongside UV and weathering resistance under the intended conditions of use.
