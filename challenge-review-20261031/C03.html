Common problems

Common Cleanability & Water Resistance Problems

The performance of a facer depends on its coating, substrate, lamination and the conditions of its intended service environment. These ten issues can affect day-to-day cleaning, moisture exposure and long-term surface quality.

01

Poor Stain Resistance

Visible stains and surface contamination can be difficult to remove from porous or rough facers, compromising the finished panel’s appearance.

Explore solution →

02

Limited Washability

Repeated wet wiping may cause visible wear, surface discoloration or coating damage on facers that are not designed for washability.

Explore solution →

03

Poor Resistance to Cleaning Chemicals

Routine cleaning or disinfecting agents can affect facer color, coating integrity or surface finish if chemical compatibility is insufficient.

Explore solution →

04

Insufficient Water Repellency

Water droplets may wet and spread across a facer rather than bead on its surface, increasing surface exposure to liquid water.

Explore solution →

05

Excessive Water Absorption

Excessive liquid-water uptake may compromise the facing or introduce moisture into moisture-sensitive insulation constructions.

Explore solution →

06

Moisture Penetration Through Facing

Water can pass through weaknesses in the facing or through defects such as pinholes, tears and insufficiently sealed joints.

Explore solution →

07

Inadequate Vapor Permeability / Barrier Performance

A vapor-open assembly can be impaired by an overly restrictive facing, while vapor-control systems may fail if the facing transmits too much moisture vapor.

Explore solution →

08

Moisture-Induced Surface Damage

After moisture exposure, the surface may show peeling, blistering, softening or reduced adhesion, particularly under repeated wet–dry conditions.

Explore solution →

09

Mold & Mildew Risk

Persistent dampness and condensation may create conditions that support mold or mildew on the panel surface or associated deposits.

Explore solution →

10

Poor Durability in Humid Environments

Sustained high humidity and heat may accelerate coating degradation, bond failure or deterioration of the finished panel’s surface.

Explore solution →

Targeted solutions

Targeted Solutions, Problem by Problem

Each solution below corresponds directly to a customer pain point above. Material selection and performance claims should be confirmed by tests reflecting actual chemicals, exposure conditions and the finished assembly.

01

Reduce Dirt Pickup with an Easy-Clean Surface

Customer problem

Poor Stain Resistance

Visible stains and surface contamination can be difficult to remove from porous or rough facers, compromising the finished panel’s appearance.

GRECHO solution

Use a low-soiling, easy-clean coating and adjust the surface pore structure and roughness. Confirm stain resistance with representative contaminants and cleaning procedures.

Related materials

Washable Fiberglass Coated Veil

02

Improve Coating Integrity Under Repeated Wiping

Customer problem

Limited Washability

Repeated wet wiping may cause visible wear, surface discoloration or coating damage on facers that are not designed for washability.

GRECHO solution

Optimize coating resin selection, crosslinking and adhesion; then evaluate performance through repeated wet-wipe cycles under realistic use conditions.

Related materials

Washable Fiberglass Coated Veil

03

Select a Coating Compatible with Target Cleaners

Customer problem

Poor Resistance to Cleaning Chemicals

Routine cleaning or disinfecting agents can affect facer color, coating integrity or surface finish if chemical compatibility is insufficient.

GRECHO solution

Match the coating chemistry to the specified cleaning and disinfecting agents. Validate resistance at the intended concentrations and contact times.

Related materials

Washable Fiberglass Coated Veil

04

Introduce a Hydrophobic Surface Treatment

Customer problem

Insufficient Water Repellency

Water droplets may wet and spread across a facer rather than bead on its surface, increasing surface exposure to liquid water.

GRECHO solution

Apply a hydrophobic finish to increase water contact angle while retaining the vapor transport and bonding characteristics required by the panel design.

Related materials

High-Waterproof Fiberglass Coated Veil

05

Control Water Uptake or Choose a Barrier Facer

Customer problem

Excessive Water Absorption

Excessive liquid-water uptake may compromise the facing or introduce moisture into moisture-sensitive insulation constructions.

GRECHO solution

Improve fiber surface treatment and coating coverage. Where the design requires a stronger liquid-water barrier, consider an aluminum foil facer and verify system-level watertightness.

Related materials

Aluminum Foil Facing

06

Match Barrier Structure with Joint Integrity

Customer problem

Moisture Penetration Through Facing

Water can pass through weaknesses in the facing or through defects such as pinholes, tears and insufficiently sealed joints.

GRECHO solution

Choose a hydrophobic facing construction or a continuous foil barrier according to the exposure conditions; control pinholes, tears and joint sealing throughout converting and installation.

Related materials

Aluminum Foil Facing

07

Specify Vapor-Open or Vapor-Barrier Behavior Deliberately

Customer problem

Inadequate Vapor Permeability / Barrier Performance

A vapor-open assembly can be impaired by an overly restrictive facing, while vapor-control systems may fail if the facing transmits too much moisture vapor.

GRECHO solution

Use vapor-permeable facing for assemblies that need drying potential and low water-vapor-transmission foil for assemblies requiring a vapor barrier. Check the facing’s position within the complete construction.

Related materials

Aluminum Foil Facing

08

Upgrade Wet Adhesion and Test Cyclic Exposure

Customer problem

Moisture-Induced Surface Damage

After moisture exposure, the surface may show peeling, blistering, softening or reduced adhesion, particularly under repeated wet–dry conditions.

GRECHO solution

Improve water resistance and coating adhesion, use a moisture-resistant laminating adhesive where appropriate, and test the finished construction through wet–dry cycling.

Related materials

High-Waterproof Fiberglass Coated Veil

09

Combine Moisture-Resistant Materials with Moisture Management

Customer problem

Mold & Mildew Risk

Persistent dampness and condensation may create conditions that support mold or mildew on the panel surface or associated deposits.

GRECHO solution

Favor moisture-resistant surfaces with low available organic nutrient content, and address drainage, ventilation and condensation control. Validate any antimicrobial or mold-resistant treatment when specified.

Related materials

High-Waterproof Fiberglass Coated Veil

10

Validate Coatings and Adhesives for Humid Service

Customer problem

Poor Durability in Humid Environments

Sustained high humidity and heat may accelerate coating degradation, bond failure or deterioration of the finished panel’s surface.

GRECHO solution

Specify coatings and laminating adhesives designed for humid and warm conditions; evaluate cyclic humidity, condensation exposure and longer-term aging before final material approval.

Related materials

High-Waterproof Fiberglass Coated Veil

Application scenarios

Application Scenarios

Indicative application examples to connect material properties to potential use. Final technical suitability should be verified against each customer’s system and exposure conditions.

Acoustic Ceiling & Wall Panels

Specify washable facers for regularly cleaned interiors and assess appearance, durability and cleaning-chemical compatibility at the finished-panel level.

Healthcare & Educational Interiors

Where routine cleaning is frequent, prioritize repeat-wipe durability and verify resistance to the actual cleaners and disinfectants used on site.

Mineral Wool Insulation Facings

Consider moisture-resistant, vapor-appropriate facers for pitched-roof and building-envelope systems while preserving the assembly’s required drying potential.

PIR/PUR, Pipe & HVAC Insulation

Use hydrophobic or foil composite facers according to system moisture exposure and vapor-control goals; confirm seams, joints and composite durability.
