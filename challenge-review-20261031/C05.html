Common problems

Common Production Consistency Problems

Repeated disturbances on the production line can begin with small variations in facer properties. These are ten practical issues manufacturers may encounter during coating, unwinding, laminating or board production.

01

Roll-to-Roll Quality Variation

Visible differences in weight, shade, thickness or functional behavior between incoming rolls can make a stable production setup difficult to maintain.

Explore solution →

02

Inconsistent Coating Weight

Coating add-on that varies across the width or along the roll can produce uneven appearance and change downstream material behavior.

Explore solution →

03

Insufficient Roll Width

A facer that is narrower than the board or laminating line may require additional joins, roll changes or line adjustments.

Explore solution →

04

Poor Dimensional Stability

Material shrinkage or expansion under heat, humidity or web tension may affect alignment, lamination and finished-board dimensions.

Explore solution →

05

Web Breakage During Processing

Web breaks during unwinding, coating or lamination interrupt production and may increase reject rates.

Explore solution →

06

Uneven Roll Tension & Alignment

Uneven roll tension or web tracking can lead to wrinkles, misalignment and stoppages during continuous processing.

Explore solution →

07

Inconsistent Edge Quality

Frayed, damaged or uneven roll edges can reduce conversion efficiency and interfere with alignment.

Explore solution →

08

Unstable Material Permeability

Variation in air, vapor or gypsum-slurry penetration can lead to inconsistent conversion behavior or finished-panel performance.

Explore solution →

09

High Production Waste & Scrap Rate

Surface defects, web damage and converting interruptions can increase offcuts, scrap and rejected boards.

Explore solution →

10

Frequent Production Adjustments

Changes in facer behavior from roll to roll may require repeated adjustments to tension, temperature, line speed or adhesive application.

Explore solution →

Application scenarios

Application Scenarios

Production consistency matters across different board and panel systems; the most suitable material properties depend on the end product and manufacturing process.

Acoustic Ceiling & Wall Panels

Stable coating, visual appearance and roll handling support repeatable lamination and uniform panels.

PIR/PUR Foam Board Insulation

Width compatibility, batch traceability and web integrity support continuous insulation-board production.

Fiberglass-Faced Gypsum Boards

Control of dimension, edges and slurry penetration contributes to predictable board-facer integration.

Mineral Wool Roof Insulation

Consistent roll tension, alignment and web behavior support facer conversion for roof-insulation assemblies.
