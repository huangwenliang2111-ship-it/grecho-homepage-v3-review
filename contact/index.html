FAQ Preview

Common questions before submitting a request.

Concise answers for technical data, samples, report scope and GRECHO's coordination role.

Request technical data→
What should I include in a sample request?

Please provide board type, target use, grammage or thickness, width, color, coating requirements, bonding method, target standard and whether sample review is needed.

Can we request Full TDS or test reports?

Full TDS and test reports require internal review and are shared according to selected product, submitted sample, application scope and report scope.

Can GRECHO support custom requirements?

For qualified long-term volume projects, special width, coating or process requirements may be reviewed through selected manufacturing partner coordination.

Is GRECHO a self-owned factory?

No. GRECHO is a fiberglass facer solution provider and selected supply-chain coordination partner, not a self-owned factory operator.
