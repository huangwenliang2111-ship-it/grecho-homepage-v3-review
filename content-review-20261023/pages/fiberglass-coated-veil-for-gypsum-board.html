02 — PRODUCTION CHALLENGES

Eyebrow: PERFORMANCE STARTS AT THE SURFACE

What Makes a Reliable Gypsum Board Facing?

A gypsum board is only as reliable as the bond between its core and facing.

During gypsum board manufacturing, the facing material must work with the gypsum slurry, maintain structural consistency, and withstand subsequent processing conditions.

Unsuitable facing characteristics can lead to bonding defects, surface irregularities, and production challenges.

Four essential factors influence the performance of a fiberglass-faced gypsum board.

01 — Insufficient Slurry Penetration

If gypsum slurry cannot adequately penetrate or interact with the facing structure, weak interfacial bonding may occur, increasing the risk of surface separation.

02 — Surface Delamination

Incompatible facing and gypsum core characteristics can result in insufficient adhesion, potentially causing peeling or separation during processing and handling.

03 — Dimensional Instability

Facing deformation or inconsistent material tension may contribute to wrinkles, surface distortion, and difficulties maintaining board dimensions.

04 — Moisture-Related Deterioration

Exposure to moisture can challenge the integrity of unsuitable facing materials and affect the performance of the finished gypsum board system.

Small Facing Problems. Bigger Production Challenges.

Selecting the right facing material helps manufacturers address potential defects before they affect finished-board quality and production efficiency.

11 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Eyebrow: FAQ

Questions Manufacturers Ask Before Choosing a Gypsum Board Facer

Q1 — What is Fiberglass Coated Veil for Gypsum Board?

It is a fiberglass veil substrate with an engineered surface coating, designed for integration with gypsum core materials during gypsum board manufacturing.

Q2 — Can fiberglass facing replace traditional paper facing?

Fiberglass coated veil can serve as an alternative facing material in appropriately designed gypsum board systems.

However, the board formulation and production process may require adjustments to achieve suitable bonding and finished-board performance.

Q3 — How does the facing affect gypsum core bonding?

The fiberglass structure, surface coating, and permeability characteristics can influence how gypsum slurry interacts with the facing material.

Appropriate selection and production testing are important to establish reliable interfacial bonding.

Q4 — Is fiberglass coated veil suitable for moisture-resistant gypsum boards?

Moisture-resistant facing grades can be evaluated for gypsum boards intended for humidity-sensitive applications.

Final moisture performance depends on the facing, gypsum core formulation, and complete board construction.

Q5 — Can it be used for exterior gypsum sheathing?

Specialized fiberglass facing materials can be evaluated for exterior gypsum sheathing applications.

Suitability should be confirmed through relevant weather-resistance, water-resistance, and finished-board performance testing.

Q6 — Can the roll width and material specifications be customized?

Customization options may include roll width, basis weight, coating characteristics, permeability, and surface properties, depending on product grade and manufacturing feasibility.

Q7 — Can I request samples for production testing?

Yes. Share your board structure, gypsum slurry characteristics, and production requirements with GRECHO to discuss suitable facing grades and sample evaluation.
