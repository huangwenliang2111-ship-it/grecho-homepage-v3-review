02 — PRODUCTION & APPLICATION CHALLENGES

Eyebrow: WATER RESISTANCE MATTERS

What Happens When Water Reaches the Gypsum Core?

Water exposure can compromise the performance of an inadequately protected gypsum board.

Gypsum boards used in moisture-prone environments require careful consideration of their facing materials.

When the facing allows excessive water penetration, moisture may reach the gypsum core, potentially affecting board integrity, surface adhesion, and long-term performance.

Four common challenges highlight the importance of choosing a suitable water-resistant facing.

01 — Water Penetration

An insufficiently water-resistant facing may allow liquid water to pass through the surface and reach the gypsum core.

02 — Core Deterioration

Excessive moisture exposure can contribute to softening, loss of cohesion, or other changes in moisture-sensitive gypsum core materials.

03 — Surface Delamination

Moisture exposure and unsuitable bonding characteristics may increase the risk of facing separation from the gypsum core.

04 — Dimensional Changes

Moisture-related changes in the board system may contribute to deformation, surface irregularities, or reduced dimensional consistency.

Small Water Pathways. Bigger Board Problems.

Selecting a suitable water-resistant facing helps manufacturers address moisture-related risks at the surface before they affect finished-board performance.

11 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Eyebrow: FAQ

Questions Manufacturers Ask Before Choosing a Waterproof Gypsum Facer

Q1 — What is High-Waterproof Fiberglass Coated Veil?

It is a fiberglass veil substrate combined with a specially formulated water-resistant coating, designed for use as a facing material in moisture-resistant gypsum board systems.

Q2 — How does it differ from high-absorbency fiberglass facing?

High-absorbency facing is designed to take up moisture more readily, while high-waterproof facing is designed to limit liquid water penetration.

The appropriate material depends on the application and the required behavior of the finished gypsum board.

Q3 — Can high-waterproof facing help protect the gypsum core?

Yes. A water-resistant coating can help reduce liquid water penetration through the facing surface.

However, overall gypsum core protection depends on the complete board construction, including edges, joints, core formulation, and installation conditions.

Q4 — Will the waterproof coating affect gypsum slurry bonding?

It may. Coating structure, permeability, and gypsum slurry characteristics can influence interfacial bonding.

Suitable facing grades should be evaluated through production trials and bonding tests.

Q5 — Is it suitable for exterior gypsum sheathing?

High-waterproof fiberglass facing can be evaluated for exterior gypsum sheathing systems requiring enhanced surface water resistance.

Final suitability depends on the complete board assembly and relevant environmental performance testing.

Q6 — Can it replace a waterproof membrane in roofing applications?

No. A water-resistant fiberglass facing is not a substitute for the waterproof membrane required in a roofing system.

Its role is to support the performance of the gypsum cover board within an appropriately designed roofing assembly.

Q7 — Can the coating and roll dimensions be customized?

Customization options may include coating characteristics, basis weight, roll width, and other material properties, depending on the selected product grade and manufacturing feasibility.

Q8 — Can I request samples for production testing?

Yes. Share your gypsum board structure, production process, and water-resistance requirements with GRECHO to discuss suitable sample options.
