WATER-RESISTANT GYPSUM FACING SOLUTIONS

High-Waterproof Fiberglass Coated Veil for Gypsum Board

Keep Water Out. Protect What’s Within.

A water-resistant coated fiberglass facing to help protect gypsum board surfaces. Evaluate liquid-water penetration and slurry compatibility with grade-specific testing of the complete board or roofing assembly.

  • Water-resistant coating
  • Stable fiberglass substrate
  • Selectable coating properties

WATER RESISTANCE MATTERS

What Happens When Water Reaches the Gypsum Core?

Excessive moisture reaching a susceptible gypsum core can affect cohesion, adhesion and dimensional consistency.

01

Liquid-water penetration

An insufficiently resistant facing may allow water through the surface to the gypsum core.

02

Core deterioration

Moisture can contribute to softening or loss of cohesion in moisture-sensitive core materials.

03

Facing separation

Moisture exposure and unsuitable bonding can increase delamination risk.

04

Dimensional changes

Changes within the board may cause deformation, surface irregularities or reduced consistency.

ENGINEERED FOR WATER RESISTANCE

A Better Barrier for More Reliable Gypsum Boards

Selected coating helps limit surface liquid-water penetration; a stable fiberglass substrate supports coverage, handling and board processing.

Coating coverage

Review coating uniformity to reduce localized variation in protection across the facing surface.

Production compatibility

Evaluate coating, permeability and substrate with gypsum slurry bonding, unwinding, forming and subsequent processing.

SEE THE DIFFERENCE

Different Facings. Different Water Responses.

Absorbency and resistance to liquid-water penetration serve different purposes. Choose the response required by the board design.

01

Standard fiberglass facer

A fiberglass-based facing for gypsum board manufacturing may offer limited liquid-water resistance without an appropriate protective coating.

02

High-absorbency fiberglass facer

A structure intended for greater moisture uptake and selected permeability, distinct from a facing designed to restrict liquid-water entry.

03

High-Waterproof coated veil

A water-resistant coating helps reduce surface liquid-water penetration in suitable moisture-resistant gypsum boards.

HOW IT WORKS

A Thin Facing. An Important Water-Resistant Layer.

The coated fiberglass substrate forms the water-resistant surface. The gypsum core provides the primary body and thickness of the board.

Water-resistant surface coating

A selected formulation limits liquid-water penetration through the facing and provides consistent protective coverage.

Compatible gypsum core

Evaluate coating distribution, facing permeability and slurry characteristics together to achieve suitable interfacial adhesion without compromising the intended water resistance.

FAQ

Questions Manufacturers Ask Before Choosing a Waterproof Gypsum Facer

Does the facing protect the entire core?

A water-resistant coating can reduce surface penetration. Overall protection also depends on board construction, edges, joints, core formulation and installation.

Can the coating affect slurry bonding?

Yes. Coating structure, permeability and slurry characteristics affect interfacial bonding. Evaluate the grade through production trials and bonding tests.

Can it replace a roofing waterproof membrane?

No. A water-resistant fiberglass facing is not a substitute for the waterproof membrane required in a roofing system. Its role is to support the performance of the gypsum cover board within an appropriately designed roofing assembly.

What can be customized?

Coating, basis weight, roll width and other properties may be available, depending on the selected grade and manufacturing feasibility.
