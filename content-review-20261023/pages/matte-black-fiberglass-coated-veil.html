02 — SURFACE QUALITY MATTERS

Eyebrow: DESIGNED FOR DARK INTERIORS

Why Matte Black Surface Quality Matters

In spaces designed around darkness, even small surface imperfections can become noticeable.

Cinemas, bars, and performance venues rely on carefully controlled lighting to create atmosphere.

Uneven black tones, unwanted reflections, and inconsistent surface textures can disrupt the visual consistency of acoustic ceilings and wall panels.

For manufacturers, achieving a refined matte black finish involves four essential considerations.

01 — Black Color Consistency

Variations in black tone between panels may create visible differences across large ceiling and wall installations.

02 — Low-Glare Surface

Unwanted surface reflections can become noticeable under spotlights, stage lighting, and projection environments.

03 — Fine & Uniform Texture

A refined, consistent surface texture helps create a seamless appearance across adjoining acoustic panels.

04 — Acoustic Compatibility

Selectable air permeability helps manufacturers match facing materials to different acoustic cores and sound absorption requirements.

Small Reflections. Noticeable Distractions.

A consistent matte black surface helps acoustic panels blend into darker interiors, allowing the lighting, architecture, and performance to remain the focus.

11 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Eyebrow: FAQ

Questions Manufacturers Ask Before Choosing a Black Facer

Q1 — What is Matte Black Fiberglass Coated Veil made of?

It consists of a lightweight fiberglass veil substrate combined with a matte black surface coating, designed for use as the facing layer of acoustic ceiling and wall panels.

Q2 — Does the matte black surface help reduce light reflection?

A matte finish is designed to reduce the appearance of specular reflections compared with glossier surfaces. Actual reflection characteristics depend on the coating formulation, surface texture, lighting conditions, and viewing angle.

Q3 — Can air permeability be customized?

Different air permeability grades can be evaluated according to the acoustic panel design and required sound absorption characteristics.

Q4 — Is it suitable for both mineral wool and glass wool?

GRECHO matte black fiberglass facings can be evaluated for compatibility with mineral wool and glass wool acoustic panels. Final suitability depends on the core material, lamination process, and performance requirements.

Q5 — Can I request samples for production testing?

Yes. Share your core material, facing requirements, and intended application with GRECHO to discuss suitable sample options.

Q6 — Can the roll width and coating characteristics be customized?

Customization options may include roll width, basis weight, coating characteristics, surface coverage, and air permeability, subject to the selected product grade and manufacturing feasibility.
