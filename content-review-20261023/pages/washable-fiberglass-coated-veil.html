02 — SURFACE QUALITY MATTERS

Eyebrow: BEYOND SURFACE APPEARANCE

Why Cleanability Matters in Ceiling Design

A ceiling should do more than look clean. It should be designed to stay easy to maintain.

In hospitals, schools, and other frequently used public spaces, ceiling panels can face repeated cleaning, moisture exposure, and demanding maintenance routines.

Conventional facing materials may not be suitable for these conditions, potentially affecting the surface appearance and integrity of the finished panels.

For manufacturers, choosing the right ceiling facing involves four essential considerations.

01 — Cleaning Durability

Frequent wiping or washing may damage unsuitable surfaces, resulting in coating deterioration or visible wear.

02 — Chemical Resistance

Exposure to cleaning agents and disinfectants can affect the appearance and integrity of ceiling surfaces that are not designed for chemical contact.

03 — Moisture Resistance

Humid environments and repeated moisture exposure can challenge the durability and appearance of conventional ceiling facings.

04 — Surface Integrity

Poorly bonded coatings or unsuitable substrates may release loose surface material or deteriorate during repeated cleaning.

Cleaner Surfaces. More Reliable Performance.

A facing material designed for repeated cleaning helps manufacturers develop ceiling panels with more practical maintenance characteristics and consistent surface quality.

06 — PRODUCT STRUCTURE

Eyebrow: HOW IT WORKS

A Thin Facing. An Essential Protective Surface.

The outer surface plays an important role in how an acoustic ceiling panel is cleaned and maintained.

GRECHO Washable Fiberglass Coated Veil is applied to the visible surface of acoustic panels, typically over a mineral wool or glass wool core.

Its lightweight structure combines a clean decorative appearance with functional surface protection.

Layer 01 — Washable Fiberglass Coated Veil

A lightweight, dimensionally stable fiberglass veil combined with a specially formulated washable surface coating.

The coated structure provides a refined appearance while supporting cleaning durability, moisture resistance, and chemical compatibility.

Layer 02 — Acoustic Core

Mineral wool or glass wool forms the primary sound-absorbing structure of the finished ceiling panel.

Designed to Work Together.

The facing and acoustic core should be evaluated as a complete ceiling system to confirm cleaning durability, moisture behavior, acoustic performance, and manufacturing compatibility.

11 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Eyebrow: FAQ

Questions Manufacturers Ask Before Choosing a Washable Facer

Q1 — Can Washable Fiberglass Coated Veil be cleaned with water?

Yes. It is designed to support wiping and washing with suitable cleaning methods while maintaining its surface integrity.

The appropriate cleaning procedure should be verified for each product grade and finished ceiling panel system.

Q2 — Is it resistant to disinfectants and cleaning chemicals?

GRECHO offers washable fiberglass facing materials with chemical-resistant characteristics.

Actual compatibility depends on the chemical formulation, concentration, contact time, temperature, and cleaning frequency. Testing under intended conditions is recommended.

Q3 — Is it suitable for hospital ceiling panels?

It can be evaluated for acoustic ceiling panels used in healthcare environments requiring frequent cleaning and maintenance.

Suitability for operating rooms, cleanrooms, and other controlled healthcare spaces must be verified against the applicable finished-system requirements.

Q4 — Can it be used in indoor swimming pool ceilings?

Moisture-resistant washable facing can be considered for indoor pool ceiling systems.

However, the complete ceiling assembly should be assessed for prolonged humidity, condensation, airborne pool chemicals, and other relevant environmental conditions.

Q5 — Can the facing affect sound absorption?

Yes. The airflow resistance of the facing can influence the acoustic performance of the finished panel.

The appropriate facing should be selected and tested together with the mineral wool or glass wool acoustic core.

Q6 — Can I request customized specifications and samples?

Yes. GRECHO can discuss different coating properties, roll widths, basis weights, and other facing characteristics according to production requirements and manufacturing feasibility.

Samples can be arranged for material evaluation and production testing.
