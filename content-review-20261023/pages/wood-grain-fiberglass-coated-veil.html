02 — SURFACE QUALITY MATTERS

Eyebrow: THE DETAILS DEFINE THE DESIGN

What Makes a Convincing Wood Grain Surface?

A natural-looking wood finish is about more than color.

The beauty of wood lies in its subtle grain patterns, tonal variations, and natural visual depth.

When decorative acoustic panels are installed across large ceilings or walls, inconsistent printing, repetitive textures, or uneven surface finishes can affect the overall architectural appearance.

For manufacturers, creating a refined wood grain surface involves four essential considerations.

01 — Realistic Wood Grain

Flat or poorly defined patterns may lack the natural depth and visual character associated with genuine wood.

02 — Color Consistency

Uncontrolled color variations between panels can create noticeable differences across large architectural installations.

03 — Pattern Continuity

Poorly coordinated grain direction or pattern placement may interrupt the visual flow between adjoining panels.

04 — Acoustic Compatibility

Decorative surface treatments must be selected with appropriate airflow characteristics to avoid compromising the intended acoustic performance of the finished panel.

Natural Detail. Noticeable Difference.

A well-designed wood grain facing helps manufacturers create decorative acoustic panels with a more refined and coordinated appearance.

06 — PRODUCT STRUCTURE

Eyebrow: HOW IT WORKS

A Thin Facing. A Natural Wood Appearance.

The beauty of the surface begins with an engineered fiberglass layer.

GRECHO Wood Grain Fiberglass Coated Veil is designed for application to the visible surface of acoustic ceiling and wall panels.

Its lightweight structure allows decorative wood grain finishes to be incorporated into acoustic panel systems without requiring a solid wood surface layer.

Layer 01 — Wood Grain Fiberglass Coated Veil

Combining a lightweight, dimensionally stable fiberglass veil with a decorative wood grain coating, GRECHO Wood Grain Fiberglass Coated Veil delivers realistic wood-inspired patterns, customizable colors, and a consistent surface finish while supporting reliable processing during acoustic panel manufacturing.

Layer 02 — Acoustic Core

Mineral wool or glass wool forms the primary sound-absorbing structure of the finished acoustic panel.

Designed to Work Together.

The decorative facing and acoustic core should be evaluated as a complete panel system to achieve the desired balance between surface appearance, sound absorption, and production compatibility.

11 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Eyebrow: FAQ

Questions Manufacturers Ask Before Choosing a Wood Grain Facer

Q1 — Is Wood Grain Fiberglass Coated Veil made from real wood?

No. It uses a fiberglass veil substrate with a decorative wood grain coating designed to reproduce the visual appearance of natural wood.

It is intended for use as a decorative facing material rather than a solid wood surface layer.

Q2 — Can the wood grain patterns and colors be customized?

Yes. GRECHO offers customization options for wood-inspired colors, grain patterns, and selected surface characteristics, subject to design and manufacturing feasibility.

Q3 — Can wood grain facing be used with acoustic ceiling and wall panels?

Yes. Wood grain fiberglass facing can be evaluated for use with mineral wool and glass wool acoustic panels.

The facing and acoustic core should be tested together to confirm the desired finished-panel performance.

Q4 — Does the decorative coating affect sound absorption?

It can. Coating structure and facing airflow resistance may influence sound absorption characteristics.

The appropriate facing should be selected according to the acoustic core and intended panel performance.

Q5 — Is it suitable for public buildings and educational facilities?

Wood grain fiberglass facing can be considered for decorative acoustic panels used in libraries, classrooms, commercial interiors, and other public spaces.

Fire classification, indoor emissions, durability, and other applicable requirements should be verified for the selected product grade and finished system.

Q6 — Can I request wood grain samples for production testing?

Yes. You can share your preferred color, grain pattern, acoustic core material, and technical requirements with GRECHO to discuss suitable samples.
