Acoustic Ceiling & Wall Facers

Fiberglass facing solutions for acoustic ceiling and wall systems across commercial, institutional and recreational applications.

Typical performance priorities

  • Air permeability for acoustic performance
  • Surface color, texture and light reflectance
  • Paint / coating compatibility
  • Hiding power to mineral or glass wool cores

Mineral Wool Insulation Facers

Fiberglass facer solutions developed around mineral wool board production and performance needs.

Typical performance priorities

  • Bonding and lamination compatibility
  • Moisture, mold and cleanability requirements
  • Fire and compliance documentation
  • Sample-to-production consistency
View Solution →

Gypsum Board Facers

Coated and uncoated fiberglass facer options for gypsum boards, sheathing and specialty board systems.

Typical performance priorities

  • Gypsum slurry penetration and bonding
  • Air permeability and drying behavior
  • Moisture resistance
  • Fire-performance contribution
  • Handling strength and board-line compatibility
View Solution →

PIR / PUR Insulation Facers

Fiberglass facer options for continuous and discontinuous PIR/PUR board production.

Typical performance priorities

  • Foam adhesion and surface tension
  • Bleed-through resistance
  • Dimensional stability during foaming
  • Fire / calorific-value requirements
