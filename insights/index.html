Common Questions

Questions behind many technical reviews.

What information should buyers prepare before requesting samples?

Board application, target surface direction, product interest, width or roll format, document needs and review stage are helpful for early coordination.

Can GRECHO recommend one fixed facer for all board systems?

No. Facer direction depends on board type, application, surface expectation, sample review and document scope.

What is the difference between Color TDS and Full TDS?

Color TDS or selected marketing TDS may support early communication. Full TDS is product-specific and shared according to review scope.
