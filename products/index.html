FAQ Preview

Questions before requesting a route

Use construction terms consistently: a veil is the base glass-fibre web, coated means a surface treatment has been added, and reinforced means a support layer has been added. Compare standard and PFAS-related routes within the same board application and bonding path, then request evidence for the selected product and defined document scope.

Open Contact→
Can I download TDS directly from product cards?

No. Products cards route requests to Contact so GRECHO can confirm the selected model, construction and document scope before sharing documents.

Does a product route mean all certificates are available?

No. Fire, sustainability and validation documents apply only to selected tested models and matching constructions.

Why do some product cards show “View Route” while others do not?

“View Route” appears only when a reviewed Product Route page has been formally published. Other cards remain selector-level directions and continue to use Contact for technical-data and sample requests.

Need help narrowing the route?

Share the board type, surface expectation and target document scope. GRECHO will coordinate a suitable facer direction for review.
