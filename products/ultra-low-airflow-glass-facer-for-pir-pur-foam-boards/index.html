Best fit Use when this route context matches the buyer system. An ultra-low-airflow glass facer route for PIR/PUR foam boards. Final suitability depends on buyer-side foam chemistry, line speed, lamination, peel validation and production trials.

Specification Overview

Specification overview for route review

Technical review focus

Route review focus For the coated-glass direction only, Ultra-Low Air Permeability means ≤2 L/m²/s at 100 Pa when supported by the corresponding approved TDS. Do not apply this threshold to the aluminium-composite direction.
Surface fit 280-300gsm ultra-low-airflow coated glass facer for PIR/PUR roof, wall and sandwich boards; confirm surface appearance, bonding direction and board compatibility before sampling.
Representative model range GC-PF300W-LA / GC-PF300G-LA for first technical review; final selection depends on buyer-side construction.

Buyer issue Route fit context

PIR/PUR board manufacturers should review air permeability, foam-interface fit, peel stability and line continuity under the selected board construction.

GRECHO route response Treat foam-interface fit, line continuity, surface stability and defect observations as buyer-side trial results under the selected board construction, not as universal material guarantees.
What to validate Air permeability, foam-interface fit, peel stability, flexibility, dimensional stability, line continuity and board defects.

Models, material type and area weight need to match the selected board construction.
