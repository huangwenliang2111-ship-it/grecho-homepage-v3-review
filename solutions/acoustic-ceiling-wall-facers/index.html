FAQ Preview

Common Questions from Acoustic Board Teams

Can GRECHO help compare several acoustic facer directions?

Yes. GRECHO can help organize direction comparison by application, surface need, sample stage and document scope.

Can samples be coordinated before all specifications are finalized?

Yes. A concise application brief can support early sample coordination before every specification is locked.

What technical documents can be requested?

Product briefs, color TDS where relevant, full TDS by review, fire-related documents by scope and sample review notes can be requested.

What should I prepare before requesting an acoustic facer sample?

Share the board type, visible surface expectation, color or finish target, intended market and any document requirements so GRECHO can coordinate a more relevant sample direction.
